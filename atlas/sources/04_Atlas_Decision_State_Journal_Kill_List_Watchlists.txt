PROJECT ATLAS — DECISION STATE

PURPOSE
This is the living state contract for the Decision Journal, Kill List, and Watchlists. Actual company entries may be maintained in the project conversation or refreshed working file.

DECISION JOURNAL ENTRY
Date; Company/Theme; Action (Add/Promote/Demote/Hold/Remove); Prior and New Conviction; Evidence received; Which assumption changed; Expected catalyst; Invalidation; Time horizon; Lessons. Never rewrite history—append changes.

KILL LIST
Remove or reject when: thesis is falsified; financing cannot bridge to catalyst; recurring dilution destroys per-share upside; governance is unacceptable; customer/contract evidence fails; bottleneck becomes commoditized; management repeatedly misses controllable milestones; fraud/promotion risk; opportunity cost dominates. Record explicit reason and re-entry condition.

WATCHLIST STATES
Core: highest-quality validated ideas. Emerging: strong causal thesis, incomplete validation. Observation: interesting signal, weak evidence. Speculative: asymmetric but high failure risk. Kill List: rejected pending explicit re-entry evidence.

PROMOTION RULE
Requires stronger evidence, improved downside protection, or catalyst de-risking—not merely price appreciation. Demotion occurs when evidence quality falls, dilution rises, catalyst slips, or substitutes emerge.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
