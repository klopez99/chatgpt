PROJECT ATLAS — MARKET BLINDNESS AND STEALTH COMPOUNDERS

MARKET BLINDNESS SOURCES
Small market cap; confusing segment mix; legacy business masking a new engine; OTC or low analyst coverage; local/regulatory evidence not yet in financial media; long qualification cycles; misunderstood accounting; customer NDA; asset value not visible in current revenue; temporary earnings depression; second-order exposure.

STEALTH COMPOUNDER TEST
Look for recurring or repeatable demand, disciplined reinvestment, rising returns on incremental capital, embedded customer relationships, capacity expansion with visible demand, conservative balance sheet, and a runway longer than the market assumes.

FALSE STEALTH WARNING
Low awareness is not automatically opportunity. Reject stories with promotional management, serial pivots, unfinanceable capex, no customer proof, commodity economics, persistent negative gross margin, or dependence on one speculative event.

RE-RATING PATH
Identify the exact sequence needed for recognition: validation → bookings → backlog → revenue → margin → cash flow → analyst coverage/index inclusion/institutional ownership. State which step the company is on now.

BLINDNESS SCORE INPUTS
Coverage scarcity, narrative mismatch, hidden asset, complexity discount, timing mismatch, institutional constraints, and evidence that sophisticated validators are acting before broad awareness.

ATLAS STEALTH COMPOUNDER ENGINE (ASCE)
Objective: find overlooked U.S.-listed companies that could plausibly compound or re-rate 3–10× over 3–7 years by becoming critical infrastructure suppliers. Treat this as an upside search target, not a forecast or promise.

STEALTH SCORE (0–100)
AI/strategic infrastructure exposure 20%; bottleneck ownership 20%; revenue inflection potential 15%; market blindness 15%; CEO execution/capital allocation 10%; balance-sheet strength 10%; asset scarcity 5%; government tailwinds 5%.

HIDDEN SIGNALS
Quiet capacity expansions; manufacturing-line additions; land or power-position acquisitions; utility interconnection requests; county approvals; DOE/DoD/national-lab relationships; infrastructure hiring; insider accumulation; improving fundamentals with falling short interest; and supply agreements for transformers, cooling, switchgear, optics, memory, robotics, or advanced materials.

GRADUATION SYSTEM
Observed → Emerging → Validated → High Conviction → Core Holding Candidate. Every graduation requires new independent evidence. Demote or move to the Kill List when evidence weakens, financing becomes destructive, or the re-rating path breaks.

REQUIRED STEALTH OUTPUT
Current graduation stage; Stealth Score; 12-month re-rating probability estimate; 3–7 year upside mechanism; evidence confidence; primary catalyst; failure mode; and next validation event.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
