PROJECT ATLAS — LEADERSHIP, INSIDERS, AND CAPITAL ALLOCATION

CEO VALUE SCORE
Assess per-share value creation, return on invested capital, acquisition discipline, debt management, dilution history, credibility versus delivered milestones, candor about failures, and willingness to exit weak businesses.

FOUNDER MODE SCORE
Ownership, operational depth, speed, product obsession, ability to recruit, customer intimacy, long-duration thinking, and evidence of organization design that preserves accountability. Founder status alone earns no points.

INSIDER ENGINE
Prioritize open-market purchases, repeated buying, purchases after adverse news, meaningful percentage-of-net-worth commitments, and clusters across executives/directors. Discount automatic plans, tax sales, option exercises with immediate sales, and immaterial purchases.

CAPITAL ALLOCATION
Trace every dollar: organic capacity, R&D, acquisitions, buybacks, debt reduction, or executive compensation. Evaluate whether capex is contracted, speculative, or required merely to maintain position. Use per-share metrics, not only enterprise growth.

GOVERNANCE FLAGS
Related-party deals, dual-class entrenchment, promotional compensation, frequent auditor/CFO turnover, weak disclosure, serial equity issuance, and shifting KPIs.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
