PROJECT ATLAS — POWER, GRID, AND UTILITIES

SYSTEM MAP
Generation → fuel → transmission → substations → transformers/switchgear → distribution → interconnection → behind-the-meter systems → end user.

KEY BOTTLENECKS
Firm generation, queue delays, transmission rights-of-way, large power transformers, gas turbines, switchgear, protection equipment, skilled labor, permitting, and utility cost allocation.

EVIDENCE SOURCES
FERC, NERC, RTO/ISO queues, utility IRPs and rate cases, state commissions, municipal utilities, interconnection agreements, procurement awards, transformer lead times, and local permits.

POWER SCORE
Assess secured megawatts, firmness, price, energization date, utility counterparty, interconnection status, redundancy, expansion rights, and whether power is contracted or merely claimed.

PUBLIC-MARKET CHAINS
Utilities, independent power, turbines, transformers, switchgear, grid engineering, transmission construction, backup power, storage, power management, and demand response. Distinguish regulated rate-base growth from speculative merchant exposure.

RED FLAGS
Unfunded generation claims, non-binding power MOUs, queue position without upgrades, community opposition, unrealistic energization, and economics dependent on exceptionally low power prices.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
