PROJECT ATLAS — NUCLEAR AND FUEL CYCLE

VALUE CHAIN
Uranium mining → conversion → enrichment → deconversion/fabrication → reactor components → construction/services → operations/maintenance → waste/decommissioning.

CATALYSTS
License approvals, DOE awards, utility PPAs, uprates, restarts, life extensions, HALEU availability, procurement commitments, export controls, capacity expansions, and supply disruptions.

VALIDATION
NRC dockets, DOE contracts, utility filings, fuel contracts, construction milestones, quality certifications, and customer commitments. Distinguish policy support from bankable demand.

SCARCITY
Qualified nuclear manufacturing, large forgings, certified valves/pumps, enrichment, specialized labor, and regulatory licenses may have multi-year replication times.

RISK
Schedule/cost overruns, licensing, fuel availability, political reversal, fixed-price contracts, project financing, and technology maturity. Apply stage-specific probability; do not value a pre-license project as an operating plant.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
