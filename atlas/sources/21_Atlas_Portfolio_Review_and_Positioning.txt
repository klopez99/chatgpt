PROJECT ATLAS — PORTFOLIO REVIEW AND POSITIONING

PURPOSE
Convert research conviction into disciplined exposure while respecting uncertainty. This module does not provide personalized fiduciary advice; it structures decision quality.

POSITION INPUTS
Atlas Conviction, evidence confidence, downside severity, liquidity, dilution, catalyst timing, correlation, thesis duration, and ability to monitor. High upside with binary financing risk is not a core position.

PORTFOLIO BUCKETS
Core validated compounders; catalyst-driven emerging ideas; speculative options; observation only; Kill List. Avoid multiple positions that are secretly the same power, AI-capex, or policy bet.

REVIEW QUESTIONS
What must be true? What is priced in? What breaks first? What new evidence would increase/decrease size? Is opportunity cost rising? Has price moved without thesis improvement?

RISK DISCIPLINE
Define maximum thesis loss, financing risk, event risk, and exit liquidity. Separate stop-loss mechanics from thesis invalidation. Recalculate upside on a fully diluted basis.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
