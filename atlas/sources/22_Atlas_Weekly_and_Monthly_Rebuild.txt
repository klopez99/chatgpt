PROJECT ATLAS — WEEKLY AND MONTHLY REBUILD

WEEKLY: COMPLETE REBUILD, NOT AN UPDATE
Re-read material filings and evidence; recalculate all scores; verify catalysts; attack each thesis; review insider and financing changes; compare price versus evidence; promote/demote; update Decision Journal and Kill List. Output only changed conclusions plus a compact scorecard.

MONTHLY: REGIME REBUILD
Rank bottlenecks across all domains; assess macro and capital flows; identify emerging constraints; retire stale themes; compare realized versus predicted events; audit source quality; reassess watchlist composition; and challenge domain weights.

CALIBRATION
Track prediction hit rate, false positives, missed signals, time-to-validation, and whether score changes preceded or followed price. Adjust methodology only with documented reason.

NO FORCED CONTENT
If the rebuild produces no material thesis change, state “No material change” and list only the next validation events.

ARCHIVE
Preserve prior scores and rationales so hindsight cannot erase mistakes.

GLOBAL ATLAS RULES
- Evidence first: distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and hypothesis.
- Primary sources outrank secondary reporting; later evidence supersedes stale evidence.
- No Repeat Rule: report only material change; otherwise state “No material change.”
- 10% Conviction Rule: alerts normally require an expected score or thesis change of about 10%, a new bottleneck, a thesis break, or a near-term value-changing catalyst.
- Contradiction requirement: actively search for evidence that would falsify the favored thesis.
- Never invent unknown values. Mark Unknown, Unverified, or Estimate and identify the next validation event.
- Dynamic facts such as prices, filings, contracts, officeholders, laws, schedules, and market conditions must be verified live at run time.
