# Project Atlas Kill List

Preserve rejected theses so a persuasive narrative cannot silently recycle them.

| Asset / thesis | Date killed | Prior verdict / score | Primary rejection reason | Evidence that killed it | Re-entry condition | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rejection reason taxonomy
- No real bottleneck
- Signal already priced in
- Weak evidence quality
- Excessive dilution or financing gap
- Balance-sheet fragility
- Management credibility failure
- Policy reversal
- Contract / permit / interconnection failure
- Implementation delay
- Market too small
- Unit economics failure
- Technology substitution
- Better public-market expression exists
- Liquidity / governance risk
- Thesis deadline expired
- Opportunity cost

## Re-entry rule
A killed thesis returns only after its documented re-entry condition is satisfied by new, independently verifiable evidence. Repeated narrative without new evidence is not re-entry evidence.
