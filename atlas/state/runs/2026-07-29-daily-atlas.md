## 1. What changed

### CORZ — AMD infrastructure partnership clears the materiality threshold
Core Scientific and AMD announced a partnership under which AMD secures more than 500 MW of U.S. AI-ready data-center capacity beginning in 2027, with the ability to expand to 2.5 GW. Core Scientific disclosed that the arrangement is anchored by 15-year agreements for approximately 530 MW across five sites and more than $14 billion of potential base contracted revenue. Total leased customer power capacity rises to approximately 1.1 GW and more than $24 billion of potential contracted revenue.

This is direct customer validation of Core Scientific's conversion from crypto-oriented infrastructure toward high-density AI colocation. It materially strengthens Validator, Re-rating, Scarcity, and Inflection Catalyst scores.

### Theme validation — off-balance-sheet/private capital is becoming a core AI buildout mechanism
Meta and BlackRock announced a venture to finance and operate a data-center campus in El Paso. This validates a broader Atlas signal: hyperscalers increasingly use infrastructure funds and project debt to accelerate AI capacity while limiting direct balance-sheet burden. This supports continued capital flow into powered land, construction, electrical infrastructure, and long-duration leased capacity.
