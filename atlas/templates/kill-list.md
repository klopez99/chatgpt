# Project Atlas Kill List

Use this file to preserve rejected theses and prevent narrative recycling.

| Asset / thesis | Date killed | Prior verdict | Primary rejection reason | Evidence that killed it | Re-entry condition | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rejection reason taxonomy

- No real bottleneck
- Signal already priced in
- Weak evidence quality
- Excessive dilution
- Balance-sheet fragility
- Management credibility failure
- Policy reversal
- Contract / permit failure
- Implementation delay
- Market too small
- Unit economics failure
- Better public-market expression exists
- Liquidity / governance risk
- Thesis deadline expired
- Opportunity cost

## Rule

A killed thesis may return to active research only when its documented re-entry condition is satisfied by new, independently verifiable evidence.
