What Oasis-E2 is

Oasis-E2 is a bedside worksheet for licensed home-health clinicians. A clinician fills out a start-of-care OASIS-E2 assessment once on an iPad or iPhone, reviews it, and exports a file that can be used for manual entry into the agency's record system (for example Kinnser/WellSky). The assessing clinician reviews and signs all content. Oasis-E2 is a workflow aid. It is not clinical advice, not an electronic medical record, and it does not submit anything to CMS, Kinnser, WellSky, or any other system.

What data the app handles

Anything you type or paste into the worksheet — which may include patient identifiers and health information (protected health information, or PHI) — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. The current version has no user account, no cloud sync, and no server that receives your worksheets. We (the operator) do not receive, see, or store your visit data.

Exports

When you choose to export, the app creates files (JSON, PDF and/or a printable page) on your device. Where those files go after that — Files, email, AirDrop, your agency's system — is your choice and is outside the app's control. A warning appears before exporting when common patient identifiers are present.

What we do not do

  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics in the app.
  • No automatic upload of visit data anywhere.
  • No connection to Kinnser, WellSky, CMS, or any EHR.

HIPAA and your agency

If you use Oasis-E2 with real patients, you do so on behalf of your home-health agency (the covered entity). PHI on the device and in exported files must be handled under your agency's HIPAA policies and any Business Associate Agreement (BAA) your agency has in place. Use a device passcode and auto-lock, avoid screenshots of real charts, and follow your agency's rules for backups, lost devices, and exported files. Note that device-level backups (for example iCloud device backup), if enabled on your device, are controlled by you and your device settings, not by the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete a visit draft inside the app, clear the active worksheet, or delete the app to remove all of its local data from the device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

Oasis-E2 is intended for licensed clinicians and is not directed to children.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, sync, crash reporting, or any data transmission, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
