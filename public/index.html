The rule is better than another major cut—but it does not resolve the payment fight

CMS proposes a 2.4% aggregate increase in Medicare payments to home health agencies for 2027 and does not propose another permanent PDGM behavioral reduction. However, the 3% temporary recoupment adjustment remains, and CMS still identifies a large historical overpayment balance.

Positive signal

CMS declined to impose another permanent behavioral adjustment despite showing an illustrative −4.062% calculation using newer claims.

Unresolved pressure

The proposed 3% temporary adjustment would collect only part of the estimated $4.9 billion balance identified to date.

Clinical opening

CMS explicitly describes home health as a potential setting for palliative care during serious or progressive illness and before hospice.

What matters most for your role

OASIS becomes a quarterly operational deadline

Beginning with the CY 2027 HH QRP, CMS proposes requiring OASIS submissions and necessary corrections no later than the 15th day of the second month following each calendar quarter.

Palliative-skilled care may fit inside the existing home health benefit

CMS is not proposing a separate palliative home health benefit. It is reinforcing that eligible beneficiaries can receive palliative services under the existing benefit when care is ordered, reasonable, necessary, and sufficiently skilled.

CMS-supported concepts

  • Medication and symptom management for pain, anxiety, constipation, nausea, or dyspnea
  • Monitoring therapeutic response and adverse effects
  • Caregiver education and symptom-management training
  • Assessment of caregiver burden and psychosocial distress
  • Nonpharmacologic symptom strategies and functional preservation
  • Advance-care planning and discussion of hospice transition

Key coverage principle

A patient does not have to be terminal, stop life-prolonging treatment, or demonstrate full restoration potential. The deciding issue remains whether skilled services are reasonable and necessary for the individual patient.

Documentation example

SN required for skilled assessment and management of progressive symptoms, medication effectiveness and adverse effects, caregiver recognition of decline, nonpharmacologic symptom strategies, and timely practitioner coordination to reduce avoidable emergency utilization.
