# Follow-Up Authorization / Get Auth Template

Use this for Kaiser/UM-style follow-up authorization requests. Keep it plain-text and fax-friendly.

```text
FOLLOW-UP AUTH [DATE]
EPISODE DATE: [MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY]

PRIMARY DX:
[Primary diagnosis/problem requiring home health]

SECONDARY DX:
[Relevant comorbidities]

DX RELATED TO HH:
[Diagnoses directly supporting skilled home health need]

TEACHABLE CAREGIVER WHO/WHEN:
[YES/NO/LIMITED. Name/relationship if appropriate. Describe exact availability and limitations.]

PLACE OF SERVICE:
[Home / B&C / ALF / Other]

SN FREQUENCY:
[Example: 1w9 + 2 PRN]

# VISITS REQUESTING:
[Number of visits, thank you.]

PRIOR LEVEL OF FUNCTION:
[Baseline assist level, DME, mobility, ADL/IADL dependence]

CURRENT LEVEL OF FUNCTION:
[Current assist level, DME, mobility, ADL/IADL dependence, changes]

JUSTIFICATION FOR SKILLED NURSING CARE:
[Why licensed nursing is required. Tie to condition, risk, assessment/intervention needs, caregiver limitations, wound/catheter/medication/disease-management needs, and safety concerns.]

GOALS AND CURRENT PROGRESS TOWARDS GOALS:
1. [Specific measurable goal] — [__% met as of MM/DD/YYYY].
2. [Specific measurable goal] — [__% met as of MM/DD/YYYY].
3. [Specific measurable goal] — [__% met as of MM/DD/YYYY].

PLAN OF DISCHARGE:
[Discharge when goals met, condition stable, caregiver/patient independent or appropriate long-term support in place, no further skilled need.]

IS THIS A HH+ PATIENT:
[YES/NO/UNKNOWN]
```

## Goal-writing rules

Avoid vague goals such as:

- Wound to heal.
- Blood sugar to stabilize.
- Call physician if vital signs abnormal.

Prefer goals like:

```text
Patient/caregiver will verbalize 3 signs/symptoms of infection and when to notify MD/SN — 50% met as of [date], requires reinforcement.

Patient/caregiver will demonstrate Foley drainage bag positioning below bladder level and verbalize catheter infection prevention precautions — 60% met as of [date], requires reinforcement due to caregiver limitations.

Patient/caregiver will verbalize CHF zone symptoms, daily weight parameters, and when to notify MD/SN — 40% met as of [date], requires continued SN teaching.
```

## Common justification language

Use only when supported by patient facts.

```text
Continued SN is medically necessary for skilled assessment, disease process teaching, medication review, monitoring for complications, care coordination, and reinforcement of safety precautions due to [condition/risk]. Patient remains homebound due to [mobility/assist/DME/fall risk/medical limitation].

Caregiver support is limited to [specific tasks]. Skilled nursing remains required because caregiver is unable/not permitted to perform [skilled task], and patient remains at risk for [complication].
```
